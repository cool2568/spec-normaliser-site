Polycab G2G TOPCon vs Waaree Elite BiN-08

Compared with the Waaree Elite BiN-08, the Polycab G2G TOPCon has a 13% lower temperature coefficient of Isc (0.04 %/°C against 0.046 %/°C), a 4.7% higher NMOT or NOCT (45 °C against 43 °C) and a 3.8% higher weight (33.2 kg against 32 kg).
