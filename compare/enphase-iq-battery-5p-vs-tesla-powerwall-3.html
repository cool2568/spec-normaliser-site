Enphase IQ Battery 5P vs Tesla Powerwall 3

Compared with the Tesla Powerwall 3, the Enphase IQ Battery 5P has a 71% lower max continuous current (13.9 A against 48 A), a 49% lower weight (66.3 kg against 130 kg) and a 50% higher design life or warranty (15 years against 10 years).
